Unto Dust
by Melchior Dahrk & Greatness7

"...unto dust shalt thou return."
- Genesis 3:19b
==============================
VERSION 2.0
Mod is now compatibile with OpenMW! The mod has been converted to a shader-based implementation (still uses the unused dust texture). This is more optimized for performance (no flipbook texture or particle system). The mod also includes options which allow you to edit the dust density and blacklist or whitelist interiors (blacklist takes priority).

DESCRIPTION
This mod adds slowly drifting dust motes to places where the dead are entombed. Similar to the atmosphere in Skyrim barrows. Ancestral Tombs, Nordic Barrows, and Ashlander Burials (including those added by mods) will all receive the new effect.


Requirements:
==============================
Choose the version for your engine:

OpenMW: OpenMW 0.49.0 or newer, OpenGL 3.3, and post-processing enabled.

MWSE: MGE XE 0.19.1 or newer, MGE XE post-processing shaders enabled, and an up-to-date MWSE 2.1 nightly build.


Installation:
==============================
This archive is set up to work with a mod installer like Wrye Mash. Choose exactly one engine folder.

00 MWSE - Copy contents into your Data Files directory. Enable shaders in MGE XE. Do not add UntoDust manually to the shader chain; MWSE controls its enabled state.

00 OpenMW - Copy contents into an OpenMW data directory and enable UntoDust.omwscripts in the launcher. Enable post-processing in OpenMW. Do not add UntoDust to the F2 shader chain manually; Lua controls it.
