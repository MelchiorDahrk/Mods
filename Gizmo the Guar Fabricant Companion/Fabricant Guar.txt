## Gizmo the Guar Fabricant Companion
by Melchior Dahrk


## Introduction
After winning the Runner Up prize for my Tumblr mod giveaway, monaluxstesblog requested a mash up of a "Guar and the Clockwork City / Sotha Sil". So I created a Fabricant guar and made it a well-featured companion.

You'll find Gizmo wandering around outside of Ald Sotha. How did he get there? Only Sotha Sil might know...


## Features
Metal feetsies
Deadly blade paws
Custom fabricant gura sounds
Upgrade Gizmo with augmentations!
Reward Gizmo with scratches, pets, or treats
Install a repair module to let Gizmo repair your gear
Send Gizmo to forage around the area for tasty morsels
Upgradeable storage capacity: 500, 750, and 1000 lb (250 lb before upgrading)
Check on the status of Gizmo's health, magicka, fatigue, and mood
Improve Gizmo's bioreactor so that he heals more when you feed him
Experience emotional turmoil with Gizmo when you visit the Clockwork City
Put Gizmo in your pocket with his matrix form to bring his optimism with you anywhere
Unlock Gizmo's hidden potential to transform into another more powerful but volatile form
Activate Gizmo's musk atomizer to attract nearby guar to your aid (including mod added guar!)
Enhance Gizmo's magicka capacitor to restore your own magicka or fill soul gems
Death is not the end for Gizmo once his matrix form is enabled; revive him!? At a cost...


## Playing the mod
To find Gizmo, travel to Ald Sotha near Vivec City. You'll find him wandering around outside.

[Augments] can be unlocked by performing the simple tasks that Gizmo's display panel will explain to you.
[Transform] can only be unlocked by discovering what became of Sotha Sil in the Tribunal main quest. Should still work even if you have already completed the quest. Caution: Gizmo will kill indiscriminately in this form.


## Requirements
Original Morrowind: MGE XE 0.10.1 or newer and MWSE 2.1 (build 3228 or newer).
OpenMW: OpenMW 0.51.0 with its bundled Lua scripts enabled.


## Installation
With a FOMOD-compatible mod manager, choose your game engine and SD or HD textures. OpenMW users must also register the installed data directory in OpenMW and enable the content files below.

Manual installation: copy the contents of 00 Core to your mod's data directory. Copy the contents of either 01 MWSE or 01 OpenMW into the same directory. For HD textures, overwrite Textures with 02 HD Textures/Textures.

Morrowind/MWSE: enable Fabricant Guar.ESP.

OpenMW: add the mod data directory through the launcher or an openmw.cfg data entry, then enable these files in order:
content=Fabricant Guar.ESP
content=Fabricant Guar - OpenMW.esp
content=Fabricant Guar.omwscripts
